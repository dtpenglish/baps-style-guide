# Editorial

Conventions for prose across BAPS DTP publications — how we sound on the page, how we capitalise, and how we punctuate. These rules apply primarily to English-language editorial work; for transliterated terms, see [Diacritics & Transliteration](../diacritics/index.md).

## Pages in this section

- [Capitalisation](capitalisation.md) — when to capitalise titles, terms, and proper nouns
- [Punctuation](punctuation.md) — quotation marks, dashes, ellipses, and series

## Quick reference

| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| Oxford comma? | Yes, always. |
| Single or double quotes? | Double for direct speech and quotations; single for quotes within quotes. |
| Em dash or en dash? | Em dash (—) for parenthetical breaks, en dash (–) for ranges. |
| Spell out numbers? | Words for one through nine; numerals for 10 and above. Always numerals for ages, dates, percentages, measurements. |

For each rule, see the detailed pages linked above.

!!! note "Scope"
    This section covers English editorial style. Gujarati and Hindi editorial conventions follow the practices of our respective language teams; cross-language rules (transliteration, diacritics, term selection) are documented in [Diacritics & Transliteration](../diacritics/index.md).
