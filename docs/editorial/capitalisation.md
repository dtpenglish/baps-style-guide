# Capitalisation

!!! info "Draft page"
    This page is a placeholder. Add specific rules as the team agrees on them.

## General principle

Capitalise sparingly. Use capitals for proper nouns, sentence beginnings, and a small set of religiously significant terms. Avoid the temptation to capitalise every important word.

## Always capitalised

- **Personal names and titles when used as names:** Bhagwan Swaminarayan, Aksharbrahman Gunatitanand Swami, Pramukh Swami Maharaj, Mahant Swami Maharaj.
- **Place names:** Loj, Vartal, Gadhada, Sarangpur.
- **Names of texts:** Vachanamrut, Shikshapatri, Swamini Vato.
- **Names of organisations:** BAPS Swaminarayan Sanstha.

## Capitalised in religious contexts (under discussion)

- *Akshar*, *Purushottam*, *Param Ekantik Bhakta*, *Brahmaswarup*

## Lowercase

- Generic terms: *sadhu*, *temple*, *scripture*, *discourse*.
- Generic religious actions: *darshan*, *arti*, *katha*.
- Adjectival forms: *the Swaminarayan tradition*, but *swaminarayan philosophy* (as a descriptor).

## Title case in headings

Use **sentence case** for headings throughout this guide and our digital publications: capitalise only the first word and proper nouns. For book and chapter titles in print publications, follow the publication's established style — but record the choice somewhere this guide can link to.

## Open questions

- *Bhagwan* / *bhagwan*: always capitalised, or only when prefixing a name?
- Honorifics like *Shri* / *Sri*: capitalised, italicised, or both?
- Pronouns referring to Bhagwan Swaminarayan: capitalised (*He*, *Him*) or not?
