# 10.3 Gurus & Honorifics
Open questions on naming and addressing Bhagwan Swaminarayan, Aksharbrahma Gunatitanand Swami, the gurus of the BAPS *parampara*, and the sadhus.

## 10.3.1 Bhagwan Swaminarayan
How to address him and what to use in which situations…

Variations in current use:

- Bhagwan Swaminarayan
- Swaminarayan Bhagwan
- Shriji Maharaj
- Sahajanand Swami
- Shri Hari / Shrihari
- manifest / incarnate
- Parabrahma
- Paramatma

Open questions:

- Which form is preferred for first mention vs subsequent mentions?
- *Shri Hari* (two words) or *Shrihari* (one)?
- *manifest* vs *incarnate* — are these synonymous in our usage?

## 10.3.2 Aksharbrahma Gunatitanand Swami
Variations in current use:

- Aksharbrahma Gunatitanand Swami (Maharaj)
- Akshar
- Akshardham
- Brahmadham
- Brahmamahol

Open questions:

- Is *Maharaj* part of the standard form, or optional?
- *Akshar* / *Aksharbrahma* / *Aksharbrahman* — preferred form?
- When referring to the abode, *Akshardham* vs *Brahmadham* vs *Brahmamahol*?

## 10.3.3 Murti Forms
Variations in current use:

- Thakorji
- Lalji Maharaj
- Shri Harikrishna Maharaj
- Shri Gunatitanand Swami (Maharaj)
- Shri Akshar-Purushottam Maharaj

Open questions:

- Hyphenated *Akshar-Purushottam* or *AksharPurushottam* or *Akshar Purushottam*?

## 10.3.4 The Brahmaswarup Gurus
Standard form in current use:

- Brahmaswarup Bhagatji Maharaj
- Brahmaswarup Shastriji Maharaj
- Brahmaswarup Yogiji Maharaj
- Brahmaswarup Pramukh Swami Maharaj

Open questions:

- *Brahmaswarup* always present on first mention? Always on every mention? Optional?
- For the current guru, is *Pragat Brahmaswarup* the prefix used during his lifetime?

## 10.3.5 Pragat Brahmaswarup Mahant Swami Maharaj
Variations in current use for Mahant Swami Maharaj:

- Pragat Brahmaswarup Mahant Swami Maharaj
- Guruhari / *guruhari* Mahant Swami Maharaj
- manifest / pragat form of Bhagwan Swaminarayan
- manifest / pragat form of Aksharbrahma
- Pragat Brahmaswarup Param Pujya Mahant Swami Maharaj
- Param Pujya Mahant Swami Maharaj
- Pujya Mahant Swami Maharaj
- Swamishri
- Param Pujya Swamishri
- Swami Bapa
- Swamibapa

Open questions:

- *Guruhari* (capital, roman) or *guruhari* (italic) — which scope?
- *Swami Bapa* (two words) or *Swamibapa* (one word)?
- When to use *manifest*, when *pragat*?

## 10.3.6 Anglophone Honorifics
Variations in current use:

- His Holiness Pramukh Swami Maharaj
- His Divine Holiness Pramukh Swami Maharaj

Open question: which is preferred, and is it used in body text or only in formal contexts (citations, dedications)?

## 10.3.7 Sadhus
Variations in current use for senior sadhus:

- Sadguru Param Pujya Dr Swami (Swayamprakash / das / ji Swami)
- Param Pujya Dr Swami
- Pujya Dr Swami
- Pujya Narayanmuni / das / ji Swami
- Kothari Pujya Jnaneshwar / das / ji Swami / the *kothari* of Sarangpur Mandir
- Mahant Param Pujya Anandswarup / das / ji Swami / the mahant
- Sadhu Jnanprasaddas, Sadhu Yogiswarupdas / sadhus Jnanprasaddas and Yogiswarupdas

Variations in current use for collective references:

- Senior sadhus
- Senior swamis
- Sadgurus / *sadgurus*

Open questions:

- *‑das* / *‑ji* / *‑das‑ji* — which suffix on first mention vs subsequent?
- Position-titles (*kothari*, *mahant*) — italicized or roman; capitalized or lowercase?
- *Sadgurus* (capital, roman) vs *sadgurus* (italic) — when?
