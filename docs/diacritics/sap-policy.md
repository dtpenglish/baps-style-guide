# 5.2 SAP Diacritics Policy
The diacritics policy for **publications for the general reader**.

For the in-house **macron-only convention** used in glossary work and editorial reference material, see [Macron-Only Convention](macron-convention.md). The two policies are scoped differently – see [Diacritics & Transliteration](index.md) for the conflict and how to choose.

## 5.2.1 The Rule
> **In publications for general readers do not use diacritical marks in the headings, text, or legends. Diacritics are not preferred in prose text. They may be used in transliterations of bhajans, scripture verses, *shlokas*, and *padas*.**

That is:

| Element | Diacritics? |
|---|---|
| Headings | No |
| Body / prose text | No |
| Photo captions, footnotes, legends | No |
| Personal names, place names, organization names, titles of works | No – ever |
| **Bhajan and scripture-verse transliterations** (*shloka*, *pada*) | **Yes – diacritics may be used** |

## 5.2.2 Names – Never with Diacritics
> Diacritics should not be added to **personal names**, **place names**, **names of political parties and organizations**, or **titles of books and articles**. These words should be spelled in accordance with the SAP transliteration system, but **without diacritics**.

Examples:

- Bhagwan Swaminarayan (not *Bhagwān Swāminārāyaṇ*)
- Sarangpur (not *Sāraṅgpur*)
- BAPS Swaminarayan Sanstha (not *BAPS Swāminārāyaṇ Sansthā*)
- *Vachanamrut* (the title of the work – not *Vachanāmṛt*)

## 5.2.3 Verses – Diacritics May Be Used
For transliterations of **bhajans, *shlokas*, *padas*, and other scripture-verse content**, diacritics may be used. This is where IAST or another diacritic system is appropriate, because the precision matters for prosody, recitation, and scholarly cross-reference.

The **BAPS in-house default for verse transliterations** is the [Macron-Only Convention](macron-convention.md) – long *a* marked with a macron (**ā**) and no other diacritics. Choice of a different system (full IAST, etc.) is a project-level decision and is documented in [Open Discussions: Transliteration](../discussions/transliteration.md).

The transliterated form used in a verse is recorded in the [glossary's](glossary-reference.md) `diacriticSpelling` column. The **same word in prose** uses the plain-Roman headword from the glossary's `term` column.

## 5.2.4 How This Interacts with the Rest of the Guide
- The rule above – "no diacritics in general text" – applies to SAP publications aimed at general readers.
- The [Macron-Only Convention](macron-convention.md) is an internal convention used in the [BAPS Glossary](glossary-reference.md) and in editorial reference material where pronunciation needs to be marked unambiguously.
- These two are not in direct conflict because their **scopes are different** – but a publication that mixes the two needs a single rule. See [Diacritics & Transliteration](index.md) for the discussion.
